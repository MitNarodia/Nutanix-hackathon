The Implementation Sequence

The Engine (Chunker & Merkle Tree): Slicing files and generating cryptographic identities.

The Vault (Storage): The atomic CAS flat-file writer and the bbolt metadata index.

The Highway (Data Plane): The HTTP zero-copy server with HMAC-SHA256 authentication.

The Brains (Control Plane): SWIM Gossip UDP and gRPC diffing.

The Orchestrator: fsnotify file watching and tying it all together.
